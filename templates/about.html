◈ HOW IT WORKS

Typing rhythm as
a second factor.

A password proves you know a secret. Keystroke dynamics proves you are the person who normally types it. Sentinel measures the millisecond timing of your typing, builds a statistical profile of it, and scores every later attempt against that profile.

What gets measured

You type the phrase {{ phrase }}. The browser records the timestamp of every key press and release — never the characters themselves on their own — and turns them into {{ n_features }} timing features.

H
Hold time
How long a single key stays down — press to release. Also called dwell. One value per key in the phrase.
DD
Down-down
The gap between pressing one key and pressing the next. Captures your overall pace through a digraph.
UD
Up-down
The gap between releasing one key and pressing the next. Goes negative when a fast typist overlaps keys.

How a decision is made

Every step below runs on the server. The browser only captures raw timings — it never scores, and it never decides.

1
Enroll
You type the phrase {{ required_samples }} times. Sentinel takes the mean and standard deviation of each timing feature across those repetitions — that pair is your profile.
2
Score
A new attempt is compared with the scaled-Manhattan distance: for each feature, how far it lands from your mean, divided by how much you normally vary on it. Features you are consistent about count for more. The distances are summed into one number.
3
Compare against your threshold
Your accept boundary is mean + {{ "%.1f"|format(threshold_k) }}σ of your own leave-one-out enrollment scores — so it is calibrated to you, not to a global constant. A steady typist gets a tight threshold; an erratic one gets a looser one.
4
Decide in three tiers
At or under the threshold is accepted. Up to ×{{ "%.2f"|format(suspicious_margin) }} of it is suspicious — close enough to be you having an off day, close enough to be someone else. Beyond that is rejected.
5
Detect intrusion
{{ fail_lock_streak }} consecutive non-accepted attempts is not bad luck — it is someone guessing. The account is flagged as an intrusion and frozen for {{ lock_cooldown_min }} minutes. A locked account is refused before any scoring runs. Verification is also rate-limited to {{ rate_limit_max }} calls per {{ rate_limit_window }} seconds per IP.

What it does not do

Replace your password
This is a second factor layered over an existing login, not a replacement for one. It raises the cost of using a stolen credential.
Work on one keystroke
The profile is built for one specific phrase. Typing rhythm on arbitrary free text is a harder, noisier problem.
Ignore its own error rate
Tightening the threshold locks out more impostors and more genuine users. That trade-off is visible on the admin analytics page, not hidden.
